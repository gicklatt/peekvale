Peekvale is in development.

The current main game build does not request or display ads. Separate advertising test builds use Google test ads. The advertising sections below describe processing when advertising is enabled. We will review this notice before the game's public release.

1. About this policy

This policy covers Peekvale: Hidden Objects and this website, provided by Gicklatt ("we", "us"). For questions or privacy requests, email gicklatt@gmail.com.

Peekvale does not require an account. We do not operate a player-account or cloud-save service, and the current game has no separate gameplay analytics or social-network SDK.

2. What the game stores

Your game progress, found objects, inventory (including coins and helpers), reward records, and preferences are stored locally on your device. These records let the game resume your progress and avoid granting the same reward twice. They are not uploaded to a developer-operated game server.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location. Advertising services may still infer an approximate location from an IP address, as explained below.

To start over, use Settings → Reset everything. This resets gameplay progress while preserving preferences such as sound settings. Removing all local app data or uninstalling the app can remove the device's saved game; operating-system backups may retain or restore a copy. Manage those backups through your device or platform account settings.

3. Advertising services

Peekvale includes Google's mobile advertising SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). When advertising is enabled, these services may process device or app identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation.

Optional rewarded ads are intended to let you earn the helper offered on screen. A reward is granted after the advertising service confirms that the reward was earned; simply closing an ad does not grant it. Clicking an ad is not a condition for receiving a reward. Interstitial ads are currently disabled.

Non-personalized or limited ads can still involve data processing. For Google's handling of this information, see the Google Privacy Policy and its mobile ads disclosures for iOS and Android.

4. Your advertising choices

Where required, Google's UMP presents privacy choices before the advertising service is allowed to request ads. Where available, open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change those choices. Depending on your location, choices, and service availability, some ads may be unavailable.

You can play without choosing to watch a rewarded ad. The current iOS integration does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. Any future tracking that requires that permission must use Apple's separate system prompt. Accepting an in-app privacy message is not a substitute for ATT permission.

You can also review advertising and privacy controls in your device settings. This policy describes data handling; reading it or playing the game does not itself give consent to optional advertising processing.

5. Visits to this website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising pixels, contact forms, or tracking cookies. Fonts, images, and scripts are served with the site, with no third-party font or video embeds.

GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operates the hosting infrastructure under its General Privacy Statement. Following an external link or opening an email app brings you to a service with its own privacy practices.

6. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send, such as a problem description or screenshot. We use it to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Email is handled through our email provider, Gmail.

Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information. You do not need to provide an advertising identifier to ask for support.

7. Children's privacy

We do not ask players to provide a name, date of birth, or other personal details to play. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, please contact us so we can investigate and address the request.

Any age-related advertising restrictions must be applied before advertising is enabled in a public release. The final store audience classification will be reflected in the released game's listing.

8. Retention, deletion, and your rights

Local game records remain until reset, removed, or overwritten, subject to your device's backup behavior. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request, related follow-up, and any applicable obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence we hold about you.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact gicklatt@gmail.com to make a request. We may need enough information to verify that it concerns you.

We cannot retrieve a local save from your device or directly erase records independently held by Google or GitHub. Their policies explain their retention practices, international processing, and request channels. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that already occurred lawfully.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as Peekvale develops or its services change. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes that require a new notice or choice will be presented as required.
