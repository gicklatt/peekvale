A few useful details

For a problem with the game, tell us your device model, iOS or Android version, game version, and what happened. The world or chapter name and steps to reproduce the issue are especially helpful.

You can attach a screenshot if it explains the issue. Please remove any personal information first. We will never need your password or payment details.

Common questions

Where can I download Peekvale?

Peekvale is still in development and is not publicly available yet. Official iOS and Android store links will appear on this website when the game is released.

Can I play offline?

The game's worlds and core hidden-object play work offline. Advertising, when available, requires a network connection. Peekvale does not require a player account.

Do I have to race a timer?

Classic mode has no timer, so you can take your time looking around. Other modes may have their own rules and time limits.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There is currently no developer-provided cloud save or cross-device transfer. Uninstalling or clearing the app's data may erase your progress, and we cannot recover it from a player account. Device backups may behave differently depending on your platform settings.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and choose Reset everything. This resets gameplay progress and inventory while preserving preferences such as sound settings. Resetting is permanent; use it only if you want a fresh start.

How do rewarded ads work?

Advertising is disabled in the current main development build. When available, a rewarded ad will be an optional offer for the helper shown on screen. The reward is granted when the advertising service confirms it was earned. Closing an ad early does not grant a reward, and you never need to click through an ad to earn it.

Where can I change ad privacy choices?

When advertising is enabled and the privacy options are available for your region, open Settings → Ad privacy choices. See the privacy policy for details about the advertising services and your choices.

How can I make a privacy request?

Email gicklatt@gmail.com with “Peekvale privacy” in the subject. Please explain the request without including unnecessary sensitive information. Our privacy policy explains local data, support correspondence, and third-party services.
